Activity 1.1.1 - CIA Triad Card Game

Information States and Characteristics — drag a card onto the matrix cell where it belongs, or click a card then click a cell. Click/drag a placed card back to the bank to move it.

Cards to place (9)

🔒 Confidentiality Ensuring access to information is restricted only to users, processes, or devices that are authorized. 🔎 Integrity Ensuring that information is a true and accurate representation of data; prevention of unauthorized modification or destruction. 🪪 Availability Ensuring reliable access to information by authorized users, processes, or devices.

Example: "Javier's Concern" — he wants to encrypt his hard drive. Encryption restricts access to those who need it (Confidentiality), and the data lives on his hard drive (Storage). So it belongs in the Confidentiality × Storage cell.
